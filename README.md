# RashmiCreativesArtSpace 🎨✨

Official web platform for **RashmiCreativesArtSpace** — Designing, Industrial Printing, Physical Branding & Digital Marketing solutions based in Malad East, Mumbai.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://rashmicreatives-artspace.vercel.app](https://rashmicreatives-artspace.vercel.app)

## 🌟 Key Features & Deliverables

- **5 Specialized Divisions:**
  - **Designing:** Logo design, corporate stationery, brochures, packaging, social media graphics, menus, invitations.
  - **Industrial Printing:** Star & Blackout flex, 3M vinyl, ACP 3D letter boards, acrylic LED boards, glow signboards, rollup standees.
  - **Office Printing:** 350+ GSM velvet visiting cards, employee RFID ID cards, serialized GST bill books & invoice registers.
  - **Environmental Branding:** Retail shopfronts, corporate office murals, vehicle wraps, exhibition kiosks, 3D reception signage.
  - **Digital Growth:** Social media marketing, Google & Meta ads, SEO, custom web applications, WhatsApp Business API.
- **Interactive Instant Quote Estimator:** Live calculation engine based on material, sq.ft dimensions, and quantity with 1-click WhatsApp quote dispatch.
- **GST Quotation & Invoice Slip Generator:** Built-in modal tool to generate branded proforma quotations with automated 18% GST calculation, print/save as PDF support, and WhatsApp sharing.
- **Categorized Work Done Showcase (34 Projects):** Full-screen lightbox preview with filter tabs (Signage, Vinyl & Flex, Branding, Stationery) and 1-tap WhatsApp inquiry per project.
- **In-House Manufacturing Facility:** Details on Eco-Solvent plotters, laser CNC routing, acrylic vacuum bending, and direct factory pricing.
- **Why Choose Us:** 4 core value pillars highlighting zero broker markups, 24-48h turnaround, photorealistic color fidelity, and on-site mounting across Mumbai.
- **Google Reviews & FAQ Accordion:** Verified customer testimonials with 4.9 ★ rating and technical FAQs covering turnaround times, file formats (.CDR, .AI, .PDF), and pan-India shipping.
- **Direct Connect & Inquiries:** Interactive contact inquiry form, one-touch call, WhatsApp integration, and Instagram link.
